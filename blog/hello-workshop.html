Hello from the workshop

I'm Riain. I studied Electrical Engineering at Northumbria University, and the parts I enjoyed most were the ones where hardware and code meet: writing firmware, wiring up sensors, and getting something physical to behave the way I'd planned.

I've wanted a place to write those projects up properly for a while, so this is it.

What I'm building right now

My main project is running AI models locally on a Qualcomm-powered Arduino board. No cloud, no server, just the board doing the thinking itself. I'll share how it's going here, including the parts that don't work first time.
